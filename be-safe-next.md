**BE-SAFE Next** (*BicyclE SAFEty powered by AI and NEXT-gen Communication Toward Cognitive Cities*) builds directly on the results of the BE-SAFE Starting Grant. It combines accident risk maps, cyclist behaviour profiles, sensor data from connected bicycles and advanced traffic simulations to understand cyclist–vehicle interactions and test practical safety interventions.

The project advances the consortium's prototypes from **TRL5** (validated in the laboratory) to **TRL6** (demonstrated in relevant environments). Real-world pilots in **Pamplona**, **Covilhã** and **Brașov** represent flat, hilly and mixed urban conditions.

## How the project works

<div class="wp-grid">
  <div class="wp-card"><strong>WP1 · Risk–Behaviour Fusion</strong>Combines dynamic GIS/ML risk maps with behavioural and near-miss profiles.</div>
  <div class="wp-card"><strong>WP2 · Advanced V2X Sensing</strong>Develops multisensor fusion, low-latency pipelines and real-time data logging.</div>
  <div class="wp-card"><strong>WP3 · Cognitive Multimodal Simulation</strong>Models behaviour, sensor interactions and safety interventions in SUMO–Veins–PLEXE.</div>
  <div class="wp-card"><strong>WP4 · Integrated Pilots</strong>Deploys and validates the approach in Pamplona, Covilhã and Brașov.</div>
  <div class="wp-card"><strong>WP5 · Open Framework & Policy Toolkit</strong>Integrates models, simulations, pipelines and datasets into reusable tools and guidance.</div>
</div>

## Expected outcomes

- validated cyclist-risk models, cognitive simulations and V2X data pipelines;
- reusable datasets, protocols and demonstration interfaces;
- policy briefs for partner cities and guidance for municipalities and mobility operators;
- an open framework designed for transfer to other European smart cities;
- a foundation for larger European research and innovation initiatives.
