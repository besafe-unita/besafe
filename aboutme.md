BE-SAFE is a collaborative research initiative within the **UNITA European University Alliance**, bringing together universities, municipalities and mobility stakeholders to improve cyclist safety in modern urban environments.

### Mission

Our goal is to develop tools, methods and policy recommendations that make cycling safer in smart cities through data-driven risk analysis, behavioural research, connected sensing and traffic simulation.

### Consortium

The Starting Grant united four universities. **BE-SAFE Next** expands the academic consortium to six universities—UBI, UPNA, UniBS, UniTBV, UPPA and HES-SO—working with eight associated partners and three pilot cities.

### Funding

The first phase was supported through a UNITA Starting Grant. Its continuation, BE-SAFE Next, is funded through the **UNITA Advanced Grants** programme for September 2026–August 2027.
