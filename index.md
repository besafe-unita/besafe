## From BE-SAFE to BE-SAFE Next

The original **BE-SAFE** project (*BicyclE SAFEty for Mobility and Active Riding in Smart Cities*) established a multidisciplinary foundation through accident analysis, cyclist behaviour research, IoT/V2X sensing and simulation. The **UNITA Advanced Grant** now supports the next step: moving from lab-validated prototypes at **TRL5** to demonstrations in relevant urban environments at **TRL6**.
