The **BE-SAFE Starting Grant** brought together transportation engineering, psychology, telecommunications, computer science and urban planning to create an integrated approach to cyclist safety. The project was completed successfully in September 2026.

<div class="fact-grid">
  <div class="fact-card"><strong>3,807 accidents</strong>Bicycle-related records from the Lisbon Metropolitan Area analysed with GIS and machine learning</div>
  <div class="fact-card"><strong>740 cyclists</strong>Survey responses from Portugal, Romania, Italy and Spain</div>
  <div class="fact-card"><strong>2 behaviour profiles</strong>Safety-Oriented Cyclists and High-Risk Cyclists</div>
  <div class="fact-card"><strong>4 connected pillars</strong>Risk analysis, behaviour, sensing/V2X and simulation</div>
</div>

## Integrated Cyclist Safety Framework

The main project result connects spatial accident intelligence, cyclist profiling, IoT/V2X monitoring and SUMO-based digital simulation in a continuous knowledge ecosystem. This creates a route from descriptive and predictive analysis towards prescriptive and adaptive safety management.
