## BE-SAFE Next Selected for UNITA Advanced Grants Funding
{: #be-safe-next-funded }

**July 23, 2026**

We are pleased to announce that **BE-SAFE Next** has been selected for funding under the **UNITA Advanced Grants** programme.

Building on the achievements of BE-SAFE, the new project will transform its results into a real-world, AI-powered and V2X-enabled framework designed to help cities anticipate and mitigate risks to cyclists. This next phase will pave the way towards safer, smarter and more sustainable cognitive cities.

The initiative officially began with a kick-off meeting on July 23, bringing the partners together to connect, introduce the expanded team, and present an overview of the project's goals and roadmap.
