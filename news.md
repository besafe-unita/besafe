## BE-SAFE Presented at ETC 2026
{: #etc-2026 }

**September 2026 · University of Porto, Portugal**

The paper *“BE-SAFE: Initial Developments of a Data-Driven Framework for Cyclist Safety in Smart Cities”* was presented at the **54th European Transport Conference (ETC 2026)**. It brings together the project's work on accident intelligence, cyclist behaviour, sensing and V2X technologies, and SUMO-based simulation.

## BE-SAFE Next Selected for UNITA Advanced Grants Funding
{: #be-safe-next-funded }

**July 23, 2026**

**BE-SAFE Next** has been selected for funding under the **UNITA Advanced Grants** programme. The expanded consortium brings together **six universities and eight associated partners** to turn BE-SAFE's results into a real-world, AI-powered and V2X-enabled cyclist-safety framework.

The initiative began with an online kick-off meeting on July 23. Its five connected work packages will progress from risk and behaviour profiling through sensing and cognitive simulation to integrated pilots in Pamplona, Covilhã and Brașov.
